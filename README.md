Ascend is an AI-powered, profile-first platform designed to help students discover opportunities that genuinely match their skills, interests, education, and career aspirations.
Regardless of their course, college, location, or academic background, students can create a single profile showcasing all of their skills, projects, certifications, achievements, and interests. We have integrated Google Gemini's API to analyse the student’s profile against relevant opportunities and generate a personalized fit score on a scale of 1-10, along with clear reasons explaining why each opportunity is suitable.
Instead of students having to search across dozens of websites, track multiple deadlines, and manually determine whether they are eligible, the platform brings the right opportunities directly to them.
In simple terms:
Build your profile once → Let AI understand your strengths → Discover opportunities that fit you.
This transforms opportunity discovery from a search-first experience into a profile-first experience.
Key Features
•	Simple Student Portfolio - Students can create a profile highlighting their skills, projects, certifications, achievements, interests, and educational background. The platform is designed for all students—not just those from technical or computer science backgrounds.
•	AI-Powered Opportunity Matching - Using the Google Gemini's API, the platform compares a student’s profile with available opportunities and generates a personalized fit score, supported by an explanation of why the opportunity matches their profile.
•	Personalized Opportunity Feed - Internships, hackathons, competitions, scholarships, projects, and other relevant opportunities are presented in a personalized feed and ranked according to the student’s suitability.
•	Simple On-boarding - Students can get started without a complicated registration or profile-building process.
•	Verified Student Badge - Students who verify their identity through a valid college email or student ID can receive a “Verified” badge, improving authenticity and trust within the platform.
•	Wide Range of Opportunities - The platform integrates Adzuna’s API to source real internship and job listings aggregated from multiple sources across the web. Hackathons, competitions, scholarships, and other opportunities can also be manually curated and added to the platform.
•	Smart Deadline Alerts - Students receive timely notifications for approaching deadlines of opportunities they are matched with—addressing one of the most common challenges: missing a valuable opportunity simply because the deadline was overlooked.

What Makes Us Different?
Most existing opportunity platforms follow a search-first model:
You search → You find opportunities → You evaluate them → You apply.
The Student Opportunity Engine reverses this process:
You build your profile → AI understands you → Relevant opportunities find you.
The platform focuses on genuine fit rather than search speed, prior exposure, or personal networks. By consolidating relevant opportunities into a single personalized dashboard, it aims to make access to career-building opportunities more accessible, intelligent, and equitable.
Target Users 
Our primary users are students and early-career individuals looking to bridge the gap between academic learning and practical experience.
The platform is particularly valuable for students who:
•	Have limited exposure to internships and extracurricular opportunities.
•	Study at colleges with fewer industry connections.
•	Come from remote or underserved locations.
•	Are unsure where to find relevant opportunities.
•	Spend significant time searching across multiple platforms.
•	Want to explore opportunities beyond their immediate academic discipline.
•	Want to build skills, experience, and a stronger career profile.
By bringing relevant opportunities to one platform, the Student Opportunity Engine can help reduce the information and exposure gap faced by many students.

Expected Outcomes & Impact
The Student Opportunity Engine aims to make career-building opportunities more accessible, personalized, and actionable for students.
By reducing the time and effort required to discover relevant opportunities, the platform can help students:
•	Discover opportunities they may otherwise never encounter.
•	Apply to opportunities that better match their skills and aspirations.
•	Avoid missing important application deadlines.
•	Gain practical experience beyond the classroom.
•	Build stronger portfolios and career profiles.
•	Overcome geographical and institutional limitations.
•	Make more informed decisions about their career development.
Ultimately, the platform seeks to democratize access to opportunities by ensuring that a student’s access to internships, competitions, scholarships, and practical experiences depends less on where they study or whom they know—and more on what they can do and what they aspire to achieve.

One-line Vision
“Don’t search for opportunities. Let the right opportunities find you.”
