Ascend is an AI-driven, profile-first opportunity platform designed to help students discover opportunities that genuinely match their skills, interests, education, and career aspirations.
Regardless of their course, college, location, or academic journey, students can create a single profile showcasing all of their skills, projects, certifications, achievements, and interests. We have leveraged Google Gemini's API to analyze the student’s profile against relevant opportunities and generate a personalized fit score on a scale of 1-10, along with explanation why each opportunity is suitable.
Instead of students having to search across dozens of websites, track multiple deadlines, and manually determine whether they are eligible, the platform brings the right opportunities directly to them.
In simple terms:
Build your profile once → Let AI understand your potential → Discover opportunities that fit you.
Ascend transforms opportunity discovery from a search-first experience into a profile-first experience.
Key Features
•	Unified Student Portfolio - Students can create a profile showcasing their skills, projects, certifications, achievements, interests, and educational background. The platform is designed for all students—not just those from technical or computer science backgrounds.
•	AI-driven Opportunity Matching - Using the Google Gemini's API, the platform compares a student’s profile with opportunities and generates a personalized fit score, supported by an explanation of why the opportunity matches their profile.
•	Personalized Opportunity Feed - Internships, hackathons, competitions, scholarships, projects, and other relevant opportunities are presented in a personalized feed and ranked according to the student’s suitability.
•	Simple On-boarding - Students can get started without a lengthy registration or profile-building process.
•	Verified Student Badge - Students who verify their identity through an an official college email or student ID can receive a “Verified” badge, strengthening  authenticity and trust within the platform.
•	Wide Range of Opportunities - The platform integrates Adzuna’s API to source verified internship and job listings aggregated from multiple sources across the web. Hackathons, competitions, scholarships, and other opportunities can also be manually assessed and added to the platform.
•	Smart Deadline Alerts - Students receive timely notifications for approaching deadlines of opportunities they are matched with—addressing one of the most common challenges: missing a valuable opportunity simply due to missed deadlines.

What sets Ascend apart?
Most existing opportunity platforms follow a search-first approach:
You search → You discover opportunities → You evaluate them → You apply.
Ascend reverses this process:
You build your profile → AI understands your profile → Relevant opportunities find you.
The platform focuses on genuine fit rather than search speed, prior exposure, or personal networks. By consolidating relevant opportunities into a single personalized dashboard, it aims to make access to career-building opportunities more accessible, intelligent, and equitable.
Target Users 
Our primary users are students and early-career individuals looking to bridge the gap between academic learning and practical experience.
The platform is especially useful for students who:
•	Have limited exposure to internships and extracurricular opportunities.
•	Study at colleges with fewer industry connections.
•	Come from remote or underserved locations.
•	Are unsure where to find relevant opportunities.
•	Spend significant time searching across multiple platforms.
•	Want to explore opportunities beyond their immediate academic discipline.
•	Want to build skills, experience, and a stronger professional profile.
By bringing relevant opportunities to one platform, the Student Opportunity Engine can help reduce the information and exposure gap faced by many students.

Expected Impact
The Student Opportunity Engine aims to make career-building opportunities more accessible, personalized, and actionable for students.
By reducing the time and effort required to discover relevant opportunities, the platform can help students:
•	Discover opportunities they may otherwise miss.
•	Apply to opportunities that better match their skills and career goals.
•	Avoid missing important application deadlines.
•	Gain practical experience beyond the classroom.
•	Build stronger portfolios and professional profile.
•	Overcome geographical and institutional limitations.
•	Make more informed decisions about their career growth.
Ultimately, the platform seeks to democratize access to opportunities by ensuring that a student’s access to internships, competitions, scholarships, and practical experiences depends less on where they study or whom they know—and more on what they can do and what they aspire to achieve.

One-line Vision
“Don’t search for opportunities. Let the right opportunities find you.”
